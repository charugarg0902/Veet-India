Key Findings — Positive Drivers
What's working well for Veet India
✓
Efficacy on Sensitive Skin
"No irritation", "works on sensitive skin" are top positive themes, validating Veet Pure Sensitive positioning.
✓
Bikini Ad Normalisation
Phase 1 created strong brand recall. "Empowering ad" and "body positivity" drove organic UGC lift on Instagram and YouTube.
✓
Myth-Busting Digital Expansion (Phase 3)
Best quarter: Q1 2026 hit 62.3% positive and net +45.4% — digital amplification of myth-busting content shifted sentiment upward.
✓
Nykaa as Strongest Sentiment Channel
Nykaa leads with 63.2% positive and lowest negative (15.7%) — beauty-focused audience aligns well with product messaging.
Key Findings — Negative Drivers
Areas requiring attention
!
Skin Reactions on Bikini Area
"Burning sensation" and "rash" appear in 22% of negative reviews — concentrated on intimate/sensitive area application, despite product claim.
!
Packaging / Seal Quality
Broken seals, underfilled tubes, and counterfeit listings on Flipkart/Amazon third-party sellers drive ~18% of negative reviews.
!
Ad Tone Polarisation
~8% of negative comments relate to the Bikini ad being "too bold for India" — concentrated on Twitter/X and Reddit (conservative segments).
!
Reddit IN — Lowest Platform Score
Reddit India records only 47.5% positive — younger, more skeptical audience questions product efficacy vs. marketing claims.
